# Licensing and third-party material

## Suhar itself

All outlines of Suhar and Suhar Mono other than those listed below are original work generated by the
code in `src/retrofont` and are licensed, with that code, under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**
(`OFL.txt`). No Reserved Font Name is declared. No outlines were copied from another typeface.
Noto Sans Arabic and Noto Naskh Arabic were *looked at* as references for letter structure while
drawing; they are not included and nothing was traced from them.

## Third-party artwork inside every family

| Glyphs | Source | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| U+20C4 OMANI RIAL SIGN | Outline taken from the Central Bank of Oman document "Omani Riyal Symbol Guidelines V6.0" (PDF dated 23 December 2025, page 7); stored as `third_party/currency-symbols/omani-rial.svg` | A national currency symbol; use follows the Central Bank of Oman's guidelines (keep the geometry and proportions, a space between symbol and numeral, symbol to the left of the numeral, symbol as tall as the text). |
| U+20C1 SAUDI RIYAL SIGN, U+20C3 UAE DIRHAM SIGN | SVG files from https://github.com/Mulhamx4/arabic-font-merge (commit da44f34), which states they are the artwork published by the Central Bank of Oman, the Saudi Central Bank and the Central Bank of the UAE | Repository code is MIT (`LICENSES/LICENSE-arabic-font-merge.txt`). The marks themselves are national currency symbols; their use is governed by each authority's guidelines (`LICENSES/NOTICE-arabic-font-merge.md`). |

The currency outlines are used unmodified and are the same at every weight. The Omani rial sign is the
official outline (version 1.8); the artwork used before 1.8 differed from it by 0.013 % of its area.
The Saudi riyal and UAE dirham signs were not checked against their central banks' own files.
