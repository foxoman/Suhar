Suhar (صُحار) is one of the oldest ports of Oman. For centuries its harbour linked Arabia with India, East Africa and China, and sailors told the tales of Sindbad the Sailor about voyages that began here. Today the city is known for Sohar Fort, the corniche, the Friday souq and a modern port that handles more than 60 million tonnes of cargo a year.
