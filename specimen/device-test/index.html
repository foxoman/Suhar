Suhar device test

Open this page on each phone, tablet and computer you care about. Every box says what a correct result looks like. Note the number of any box that looks wrong, and on which device.

Checking whether the fonts loaded…

1 · Fonts load

Suhar · صحار نسخ · Mono

The status line above must say all nine fonts loaded. If it lists a missing font, everything below is your system font, not Suhar.

2 · Arabic joins

صُحار مدينة عُمانية عريقة على ساحل بحر عُمان، ميناء صحار والمنطقة الحرة

Letters inside each word must touch with no gaps or overlaps. The small vowel marks sit above their letters, not beside them.

3 · Mixed paragraph, same size

تقع صُحار (Sohar) على بعد 240 كم من مسقط، وكانت قديماً من أهم موانئ المنطقة. The city is linked with the Sindbad stories, ومنها أبحر التجار إلى الهند والصين.

Arabic and English should look the same size and the same darkness. The number 240 and the English words must read left to right inside the Arabic line.

4 · Suhar Naskh, text

أصل الكتابة العربية: نقوشٌ مؤرَّخة قادت الباحثين إلى أن الكتابة تطوّرت عن النبطي المتأخر.

أصل الكتابة العربية: نقوشٌ مؤرَّخة قادت الباحثين إلى أن الكتابة تطوّرت عن النبطي المتأخر.

Thick baseline, thinner uprights, tails that sweep left. The small line must stay readable, with no broken thin strokes.

5 · Suhar Naskh Display, sloping words

أصل الكتابة العربية

Each joined word should start higher on the right and step down to the baseline on the left, and the letters must still connect. If every letter sits on its own level with broken joins, this device does not support cursive attachment.

6 · Kerning after ر ز و

الحرة وردة مرور

The letter after ر or و sits close, tucked over the tail. There should be no wide gap before ة.

6b · Space between words

بحر عُمان وصحار والمنطقة الحرة يوم الجمعة، مرور الزوار في دار السلام مع قوم كرام

The gaps between words should look about equal. The gap after a word ending in ر or و must not look clearly wider than the others.

In each pair the second one must differ: hooked tails on y and Q, figures that hang below the line, small capitals.

8 · Number inside the ayah sign

۝١ ۝٢٥ ۝٢٨٦

The digits must sit inside the round sign. If they sit beside it, this device does not apply the feature.

9 · Small sizes

11 px — The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog · ملف تحرير عرض مساعدة

Both lines readable; stems even in thickness; Arabic dots distinct.

No empty boxes. The last two signs are the Saudi riyal and Omani rial; on old systems the browser may still show them from Suhar, which is fine.

12 · Returning yeh and the rial sign

In the first line the last letter of each of the first four words sweeps back to the right under the word, and in نجم and تحت the first letter stands on the head of the second. In the second line the Omani rial sign is as tall as the figures and sits to their left.
